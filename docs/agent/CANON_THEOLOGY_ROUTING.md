# Canon, Theology, and Fiction Routing

## Core separation

La Última Vigilia uses fictional/symbolic narrative to communicate real spiritual, psychological, moral, and prophetic themes.

That does not make every fictional mechanism a theological claim.

Maintain three layers:

### Layer A — Theology / doctrine

Questions such as:

- resurrection / rapture;
- tribulation;
- Antichrist / Man of Lawlessness;
- Beast system;
- Mark;
- Third Temple;
- prophetic chronology;
- spiritual/moral claims.

Route to current theology governance in the private Watchman brain.

### Layer B — Watchman canon

Questions such as:

- who The Watchman is;
- Mad Prophet continuity;
- Lone Wolf;
- Guardian;
- Conqueror;
- enemy-force identities;
- Babylon;
- locations;
- story chronology;
- unseen-world narrative mechanics.

Route to private canon/governance.

### Layer C — Public teaching / metaphor

A Field Manual, social post, website page, or quote may use Watchman language symbolically.

It must preserve the distinction between:

- biblical claim;
- project interpretation;
- fictional metaphor;
- psychological application.

## Direction of constraint

```text
Scripture / owner-confirmed theology
        ↓ constrains
Watchman story canon
        ↓ informs
Public narrative / visuals / social derivatives
```

Never reverse this hierarchy.

## Eschatology wording

Use the private brain's claim-classification system.

Do not say "Scripture explicitly says" for a project interpretation, chronology choice, historical tradition, provenance note, speculation, or unresolved claim.

## Real-world identification boundary

Do not automatically identify a living person, current government, company, AI system, digital-ID system, biometric product, or payment network as an established Beast/Antichrist/Mark identity.

If a current event is discussed, distinguish:

- verified event;
- project interpretation;
- story adaptation;
- speculation.

## Fictional enemies

The Forces of Corruption are narrative/personified representations inside the Watchman Universe.

Do not use a social or teaching post to imply that the fictional visual entity itself is literal demonological revelation unless the current theological sources explicitly support the exact claim.

## Story changes

When changing story canon:

1. enter the private brain;
2. read its `AGENTS.md`;
3. read owner decisions and relevant domain file;
4. update the proper source-of-truth file;
5. regenerate derived indexes only if its workflow requires it.

Do not establish new canon first in a public website page or social prompt.
