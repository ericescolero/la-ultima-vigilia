# La Última Vigilia Community Management Playbook

Recorded: 2026-10-04 (America/Mexico_City)  
Scope: LUV Facebook and Instagram community engagement  
Operational status: external community-growth schedule enabled; documentation remains a review proposal until this branch is merged

## Purpose and authority

Develop a real audience through useful participation and responsive conversation, consistent with LUV's Spanish-first mission of vigilance, Christian formation, discipline, and responsibility. No follower-growth result is guaranteed.

This is public social community work in `la-ultima-vigilia/main`. It is separate from the future member/community architecture in [COMMUNITY_ARCHITECTURE.md](../docs/COMMUNITY_ARCHITECTURE.md), and it does not launch or administer El Remanente.

Read root [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), [SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_MAP.md](../docs/agent/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_MAP.md), and [AGENT_LEARNING.md](../docs/agent/AGENT_LEARNING.md). Current Watchman canon/theology/production truth is in `ericescolero/watchman-universe-brain/main`; read that repository's instructions only when the conversation actually requires those domains. Do not use old `phase-1-import` guidance as a parallel authority or copy private canon into public replies.

## Brand-specific participation

- Prioritize relevant public conversations about vigilance, spiritual discipline, Christian formation, biblical study, responsible brotherhood, and the published LUV work.
- Read the public material being discussed before responding. Do not manufacture a theological answer, private canon detail, quotation, or personal testimony.
- Keep fictional Watchman symbolism and story material distinct from doctrine and real events. Fiction does not establish biblical claims.
- Stay respectful with differing views; do not shame, exploit distress, provoke theological fights, or turn a discussion into political campaigning.
- Route new theological/canon questions to the current domain authority. Escalate unresolved doctrinal or owner-direction conflicts instead of improvising.

## Before every session

1. Read this playbook, current repository instructions, and the latest owner corrections. A newer explicit owner instruction wins within its scope.
2. Verify the active Facebook Page or Instagram professional account by visible account name/handle. Never use Eric's personal profile as a fallback.
3. Check the original platform conversation and the outcome ledger for prior work. Read the full post and recent replies before acting; skip duplicates and unanswered outreach.
4. Check actual access, applicable platform restrictions, and group rules. A connection or scheduled run does not prove that commenting or liking as the Page is supported.
5. Prioritize meaningful incoming replies and genuine questions. A session may finish with no outward action when nothing useful or permitted is available.

## Relevant pages and groups

- Select by the public topic and the substance of the post, not inferred personal beliefs, vulnerability, private traits, or follower count alone.
- Use only already accessible communities that allow the brand's Page identity and the proposed interaction. Read visible rules before first engagement and recheck when they change.
- If group rules or Page-identity support are unclear, record a generic access/rules blocker and skip. Do not join or follow groups/pages, request membership, create new group posts, accept new terms, or switch to a personal identity under this workflow.
- Prefer a helpful specific comment, a relevant question, or a sincere reaction to a relevant post. No copy-paste pitch, repetitive link, mass tagging, unsolicited cross-post, engagement bait, or fabricated endorsement.
- A like is an endorsement signal. Read what is being endorsed; skip misleading, inflammatory, exploitative, or out-of-scope posts.
- Do not buy followers, trade follows, use engagement pods, manufacture conversations, or claim personal experience the brand has not had.
- No moderation, deleting/hiding comments, blocking people, advertising spend, account settings, access changes, or public commitments are included.

## Interaction quality and stopping rules

- Write in natural Spanish, address the actual post, and contribute something useful. One thoughtful exchange is better than many empty reactions.
- Be transparent about the brand identity. Do not pose as a customer, independent reviewer, local resident, partner, or unaffiliated fan.
- Do not turn someone else's discussion into an advertisement. Mention the brand or share a link only where relevant, welcome, permitted, and covered by the current authorization.
- Continue an existing conversation only when someone actually replies or asks a relevant question. Do not send unanswered-DM reminders, repeated nudges, or chase a declined invitation.
- Stop a thread when it becomes repetitive, hostile, unwanted, or outside the approved scope. Respect opt-outs immediately.
- Stop on a platform warning, action limit, CAPTCHA, identity mismatch, unclear permission, or login/access problem. Report the exact blocker; do not bypass it.
- Escalate complaints, conflict, serious allegations, sensitive personal information, account-specific support, legal/financial questions, commercial commitments, or requests requiring a new promise to Eric.
- Do not copy private conversations or sensitive details into GitHub. Use a discreet summary for Eric only when necessary.

## Recording results and learning

Use [COMMUNITY_OUTCOMES.md](COMMUNITY_OUTCOMES.md). Record only verified actions and available metrics, with local timestamp, source, and observation window. Missing data is `not available`, never zero.

- Capture aggregate followers, profile visits, substantive replies, and other available brand-level outcomes without inventing baselines.
- Keep public-post URLs only when genuinely needed for deduplication or source verification and appropriate for this repository's visibility.
- Keep private-message deduplication in the original platform conversation. Inspect conversation history before any send; if prior-contact history cannot be verified, skip the introduction. Do not export a contact list or private conversation index into GitHub.
- No private message bodies, private thread links, private profile details, personal phone/email/address, sensitive traits, login data, or bulk audience export.
- Reconcile actual results at the next relevant session. Do not claim that one comment caused a follower increase without evidence.
- Capture a Monday baseline from available metrics and prepare the weekly summary during Sunday's 18:00 session. Compare like-for-like windows, identify useful topics/conversations, and adjust quality or topic selection within scope. Report unavailable data clearly; never invent a baseline. New schedules, spending, claims, or permissions require the appropriate decision.
- Record durable owner feedback in the owning project's governing file and learning log. Do not silently redefine another brand.
